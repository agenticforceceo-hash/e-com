# Elite Professional Redesign - Design Tokens

## Aesthetic: Dark Luxury
- **Primary Background:** #0A0A0A (Rich Black)
- **Secondary Background:** #1A1A1A (Dark Grey)
- **Accent Color:** #D4AF37 (Metallic Gold)
- **Secondary Accent:** #FFFFFF (Pure White for high contrast text)
- **Text Primary:** #F5F5F5 (Off-white)
- **Text Secondary:** #A0A0A0 (Muted Grey)

## Typography
- **Headings:** Playfair Display (Serif, Elegant)
- **Body:** Inter or Montserrat (Sans-serif, Readable, Modern)

## Interactions (GSAP/Framer Motion)
- **Scroll Parallax:** On product showcases and brand narratives.
- **Hover Effects:** Subtle gold glow or scale on product cards.
- **Entrance Animations:** Fade-in with slight upward slide for section headers.
- **Interactive AI:** Floating minimalist chat bubble (gold/black).

## Layout
- Wide margins, plenty of whitespace (or "blackspace").
- Minimalist navigation with a focus on "Shop" and "Brands".
- High-resolution hero sections with professional hair care photography.
