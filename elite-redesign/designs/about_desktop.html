Our Journey

Founded in 2001, Elite Professional began with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between global salon innovations and the discerning beauty market of the UAE. What started as a boutique distribution house has evolved into an icon of hair care excellence.

Over two decades, we have curated an exclusive portfolio of brands that meet our rigorous standards for performance, sustainability, and sensory luxury. We don't just sell products; we cultivate beauty culture.
